About

Software developer. Technical leader. Problem solver.

I build practical software solutions by combining hands-on engineering, technical leadership, and a focus on solving real problems.

My Journey

From technical support to software engineering.

My path into software development wasn't centered around a single technology or job title. Over the course of my career, I've worked across technical support, automation, application development, team leadership, and AI-enabled solutions.

That experience taught me to look at technology from the user's perspective first. Whether I'm building an application, automating a process, or designing a new workflow, I want to understand the problem before deciding how technology should solve it.

Along the way, I pursued a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science to strengthen the academic foundation behind the engineering work I was already doing. The program expanded my experience across software design, algorithms, databases, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and modern full-stack development.

Today, I bring those experiences together: practical engineering, technical leadership, continuous learning, and a strong focus on building software that makes complicated things easier.

How I Work

Start with the problem, not the technology.

Good software decisions begin with understanding what needs to be solved, who the solution is meant to help, and what success should look like.

Understand First

Before choosing a framework or architecture, I want to understand the user, the problem, and the outcome we're trying to create.

Keep It Practical

I prefer solutions that are maintainable, understandable, and appropriate for the problem rather than complexity for its own sake.

Build for People

Whether it's an internal enterprise tool or a public application, the technology should ultimately make someone's work or experience better.

Keep Learning

Technology changes quickly. I enjoy learning new tools, testing new approaches, and applying what works to real problems.
